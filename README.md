An entry for the [The Funniest Excuse for Skipping a Meeting](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-the-funniest-excuse-for-skipping-a-meeting-5cecf163) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): the funniest excuse for skipping a meeting, in 200 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> I can't make the 9 AM standup — I'm booked at the bank at 9:05 to prove I'm still alive, because their system has listed me as deceased since 2019.

Reversal: skipping the 9 AM standup to go to the bank at 9:05 and prove you are still alive — because the bank's system has listed you as deceased since 2019. One concrete detail (9:05, deceased since 2019). 147 characters, within the 1–200 gate.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the single-line excuse to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`). Node 22 stdlib only, no dependencies.
